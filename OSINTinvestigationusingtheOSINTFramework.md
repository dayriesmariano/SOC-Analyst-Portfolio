# Email Breach Investigation

## 6. Selecting the Email Address Category

**Image:** `email1.png`

The **Email Address** category was selected to access tools that verify email addresses and check whether they have appeared in known public data breaches.

---

## 7. Selecting the Breach Data Tool

**Image:** `email2.png`

The **Breach Data** option was expanded, displaying several breach intelligence tools. **Have I Been Pwned** was selected because it allows users to check whether an email address has been exposed in publicly known data breaches.

---

## 8. Opening Have I Been Pwned

**Image:** `email3.png`

The Have I Been Pwned website was opened. An email address was entered into the search field to determine whether it had been involved in any publicly reported data breaches.

---

## 9. Email Breach Results

**Image:** `email4.png`

The search result indicated that the submitted email address was **not found** in any known public data breaches, meaning no publicly reported compromise was detected at the time of the search.
